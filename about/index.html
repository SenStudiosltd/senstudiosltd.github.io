About us

An independent studio from Hanoi

SEN STUDIOS is a mobile game company founded in 2026 in Hanoi, Vietnam. We create casual and puzzle games for iOS and Android and publish them for players worldwide.

Our story

SEN STUDIOS was founded by an independent developer with several years of experience building and publishing mobile games. Working as a small, focused team lets us take a game from first idea to live operation, and stay close to the people who play it.

Why "Sen"?

Sen is the Vietnamese word for the lotus, a flower that grows clean and calm from muddy water. It reflects what we want our games to be: simple, calm on the surface, and carefully crafted underneath.

What we value

  • Players first. Fair design, honest monetization and responsive support.
  • Privacy. We collect only what our games need and are transparent about it.
  • Craft. Fewer games, better made, and looked after long after launch.
