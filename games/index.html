Games

Casual & puzzle games for everyone

We make easy-to-learn mobile games with real depth: calm to play, satisfying to master, and designed for players of all backgrounds around the world.

What we make

Puzzle games

Tile, match and logic puzzles with clean visuals, clear rules and steadily growing challenge.

Casual games

Quick, relaxing sessions that fit into a coffee break, playable with one hand.

Made for the world

Localized interfaces and content, optimized for a wide range of devices and network conditions.

Free to play, fair to play

Our games are free to download. They are supported by optional in-app purchases and by advertising delivered through established partners such as Google AdMob and Unity Ads.

  • Players can enjoy the core game without paying.
  • Purchases are processed securely by the App Store or Google Play; we never see payment card details.
  • Ads are placed to avoid interrupting gameplay, and where available an option to remove ads is offered.

Our approach

Prototype

Every idea starts as a small playable prototype that we test quickly and honestly.

Craft

Promising prototypes become full games with polished art, sound and carefully balanced levels.

Test

We test on real devices and with real players before release, and fix what we learn.

Launch & improve

After launch we keep releasing updates, new content and fixes based on player feedback.
